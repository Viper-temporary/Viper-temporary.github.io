How to make my Egg in a Basket

THE Egg inside toast HISTORY

Back in 2022 I was hungry and home alone because my mom was working a long shift so I decided to use what I learn to make eggs in blanket I shall teach you the power on how to make these but be aware, it is hard

  • Things you will need!
    1. Frying pan
    2. Butter or Anti Stick Spray
    3. Eggs
    4. Toast
    5. Oven
    6. Wineglass
    7. Smokey ham
    8. Cheese (any)

    How to Make Da food!

  • Heat the oven to max and throw in 1/8 of butter and wait till it melts
  • Then as its melting get the wineglass and toast and make a hole in the center for toast
  • then throw the toast inside the pan and wait till it turns golden brown then flip it
  • Now that its flip, throw in the egg in the center of the bread
  • Using the pan, you need to hit a chef flip
  • now you're done with one part grab the little ceneter part of the bread and wrap ham around it and in between the ham and toast slap cheese inside so it melts
  • Once its done cooking You can enjoy the meal
