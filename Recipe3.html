Eggs In a Basket

An old yet, Tastey meal.

Things you'll need
  • Things you will need:
  • Frying pan
  • Butter or Anti Stick Spray
  • Eggs
  • Toast
  • Oven
  • Wineglass
  • Smokey ham
  • cheese (any)

How to make it

Heat the stove to mid and throw in 1/8 of butter and wait till it melts Then as its melting get the wineglass and toast and make a hole in the center for toast then throw the toast inside the pan and wait till it turns golden brown then flip it Now that its flip, throw in the egg in the center of the bread Using the pan, you need to hit a chef flip, now you're done with one part grab the little ceneter part of the bread and wrap ham around it and in between the ham and toast slap cheese inside so it melts
